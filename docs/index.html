About

Mirror Gold is a difficulty hack of HeartGold built using hg-engine. Each trainer battle is a double battle with custom trainer AI. The game incorporates Gen 1-9 Pokémon, with overhauls stats, types, abilities, and learnsets with the goal of increasing broader viability.

Get the latest patch on Hackdex!

Major Feature List

  • All trainer battles are Double Battles!
  • Built-in Randomizer including starters, wild encounters, trainers, abilities, and learnsets (Optional)
  • Rotie-like battle info screen (press X in battle)
  • Custom Trainer AI
  • Permadeath/Hardcore mode available
  • Level Scaling
  • Fully rebalanced gen 1-9 roster
  • Mega Evolution
  • Fairy Typing
  • QoL items: Infinite Rare Candy, Infinite Repel, Fly Machine, and more
  • Condensed but vanilla story
  • 150+ TMs and 50+ TRs available in-game
  • No longer need to teach HMs; Fly is available via Key Item after catching tutorial
  • Party heal with L Button; PC access with R Button
  • EV/IV viewer in the stats panel by pressing L/R
  • Simplified EVs and IVs
  • Evasion increases have largely been removed
  • Held items are returned to you at the end of the battle
  • Eviolite prevents regular evolution
  • Randomized trade outcomes
  • Streamlined pre-gym 1 experience for reset enjoyers (reduced tutorials and intros, running shoes right away, and more)

FAQ

  • Question: How do I change game settings? Answer: Talk to Elm's Lab Assistant in New Bark Town.
  • Question: How do I activate the in-game battle info? Answer: Press X (in battle)
  • Question: What is randomized if I opt into it? Answer: All encounters, trainer teams, abilities, and level up moves are randomized.

Gameplay Tips

  • Elm's Lab Assistant has various in-game settings for you to modify
  • Level scaling is recommended. If you opt not to use it, be careful how you use the infinite rare candy as it can make the game significantly easier
  • Press X in battle to see a Rotie-like interface
  • Press L in the overworld to heal your party
  • Press R in the overworld to access portable PC
  • Rock Climb is obtained after Chuck
  • Visit Professor Oak after obtaining 16 gym badges to unlock a hidden item
  • Ice Path and the path to Blackthorn City is no longer blocked behind the Rocket hideout
  • Fossil mons are obtained via the turning in Mysterious Fossil to the researchers in the Ruins of Alph after defeating Whitney. Mysterious Fossils are found by smashing rocks
