About

Mirror Gold is a difficulty hack of HeartGold built using hg-engine. Each trainer battle is a double battle, featuring custom trainer AI, and a completely overhauled Gen 1-9 roster. The goal of this game is to provide a fresh and challenging take on the classic Johto experience with an emphasis on strategic double battles.

TL;DR Feature List

  • All trainer battles are double battles and feature much smarter AI with better teams
  • Randomizer, Hardcore, and Challenge modes available
  • Many gameplay modernizations courtesy of hg-engine, including Fairy Typing & Mega Evolution
  • Fully rebalanced Gen 1-9 roster
  • Rotie-like battle screen (press X in battle)
  • Party heal with L Button; PC access with R Button
  • EV/IV viewer (press L/R)
  • Infinite Rare Candy, Infinite Repel, held Eviolite prevents evolution, and more
  • HMs no longer require teaching; Fly is available via Key item almost immediately
  • 200+ TMs/TRs available
  • Simplified EVs/IVs
  • Streamlined early game

Level Scaling

Mirror Gold solves the infamous Johto level curve by scaling both wild and trainer encounters based on your party level. Vanilla HGSS is fairly open about gym order. Mirror Gold leans into this a bit more. Level Scaling helps to moderate difficulty based on the way YOU want to approach the game. The challenge in the trainer battles is not about what level they are, but in what strategies and resources they deploy.

Randomizer

Mirror Gold has a built-in randomizer that should feel somewhat familiar to those that have watched the Nuzlocke Invitational hosted by pChal and PointCrow. All encounters are randomized including your starters and legends. Each Pokémon has a randomized ability that persists through evolution, and a completely randomized move pool. As your go to pick your starter, you'll be prompted to enable or disable the randomizer. This is the only time you'll be offered the choice.

Gameplay Tips

  • Elm's Lab Assistant has various in-game settings for you to modify
  • Level scaling is recommended. If you opt not to use it, be careful how you use the infinite rare candy as it can make the game significantly easier
  • Press X in battle to see a Rotie-like interface
  • Press L in the overworld to heal your party
  • Press R in the overworld to access portable PC
  • Rock Climb is obtained after Chuck
  • Visit Professor Oak after obtaining 16 gym badges to unlock a hidden item
  • Ice Path and the path to Blackthorn City is no longer locked behind the Rocket hideout
  • Mysterious Fossils are found by smashing rocks. Take them to the researcher in the Ruins of Alph after defeating Whitney
